# Ignopad Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

**Vision.** Token prices should come from the people taking part, in the open, on-chain.

**Mission.** Give new Solana projects a structured way to open a launch, bring in participants, and settle at a rate everyone shares.

## The Igno window

1. A project fixes its supply, its allocation, and the span of its Igno window up front.
2. Participants commit SOL while the window is open. Early or late makes no difference.
3. At close, total SOL committed divided by tokens allocated gives one clearing rate for the whole book.
4. Each wallet claims tokens in proportion to its share of the total.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Foundation.** Brand, website, live Solana market tape, public channels.

**Phase 2: Window design.** Specify the Igno window parameters, allocation rules, and claim flow. Publish the specification for review.

**Phase 3: Build and test.** Implement the on-chain program, test on a development cluster, and invite outside review.

**Phase 4: First windows.** Open the first Igno windows on Solana mainnet with published parameters.
