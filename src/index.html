The Igno method

Four moves, from first commitment to final claim.

  1. 01

    Open an Igno span

    The project sets supply, allocation and span up front, before a single commitment lands.

  2. 02

    Commit together

    Participants stake SOL throughout the Igno span. Early arrival buys no edge over a late one.

  3. 03

    Let one rate clear

    Once the span ends, the pot of staked SOL is spread across the allocation, giving one clearing rate for every wallet.

  4. 04

    Collect your slice

    Every wallet collects tokens sized to its slice of the pot, at that clearing rate.
